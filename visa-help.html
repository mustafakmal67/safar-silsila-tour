Simple 3-Step Pakistan E-Visa Process

Follow our straightforward guide to get your Pakistan E-Visa approved hassle-free.

01

Book Tour or Request LOI

Confirm your tour package or request an official Tourist Letter of Invitation (LOI) from Safar Silsila along with verified hotel reservation vouchers.

02

Submit Application Online

Apply directly at the official NADRA Pakistan E-Visa portal (visa.nadra.gov.pk). Upload your passport, photo, and Safar Silsila LOI.

03

Receive E-Visa & Travel

Your visa approval is issued directly to your email within 24 to 48 hours (or up to 7 working days depending on country category). Print it out and fly to Pakistan!

Required Documents Checklist

  • Valid Passport: Must have at least 6 months validity beyond intended travel date.
  • Passport-size Photograph: Digital headshot with white background (JPEG format).
  • Safar Silsila Letter of Invitation (LOI): Official invitation letter issued by our licensed travel agency.
  • Hotel Booking Vouchers: Confirmed hotel bookings across your travel itinerary.
  • Travel Itinerary: Day-by-day travel plan detailing destinations (provided by Safar Silsila).
