OUR JOURNEY

Built on a Passion for Exploring Pakistan

Safar Silsila was founded in 2019 with a simple mission: to make Pakistan's most extraordinary destinations accessible, safe, and unforgettable for every traveler.

What began as a passion for showcasing the hidden beauty of Northern Pakistan has grown into a trusted travel company serving families, couples, corporate groups, students, and international visitors from around the world.

Today, Safar Silsila specializes in curated tours to Hunza Valley, Skardu, Fairy Meadows, Swat, Kumrat Valley, Kashmir, Naran Kaghan, Chitral, and many other iconic destinations.

The Values That Guide Every Journey

Safety

Your safety is our highest priority. We work with experienced drivers, verified accommodations, and trusted local partners to ensure a smooth travel experience.

Authenticity

We believe travel should go beyond sightseeing. Our itineraries connect travelers with local culture, traditions, cuisine, and communities throughout Pakistan.

Sustainability

We support local economies, promote environmental awareness, and encourage sustainable tourism practices that preserve Pakistan's natural beauty.

Excellence

From your first inquiry to your return home, our team is committed to providing responsive communication, transparent planning, and exceptional customer care.
